About Me

October 6, 2026

Hello! My name is Juan. I enjoy the abstraction of mathematics, the algorithmic thinking and problem-solving mindset that one has to apply when writing code, and delving deep into the hidden patterns that lie within different kinds of data.

All of these have guided what I have done with my life so far. For example:

  • I have worked as a Data Scientist at Nommon, programming in Python and PySpark and developing data science solutions related with mobility and transportation.
  • I hold two master’s degrees: one in Big Data Analytics, and one in Mathematics (which I obtained while working as a part-time professor).

Some of the things I am currently interested in or working on are:

  • I want to complete my WIP Game Boy emulator, using it as an excuse to learn some more Go (some day…)
  • I recently bought a PineTime watch dev kit. I would like to get into some embedded development and using it to undestand more about how these devices work.
